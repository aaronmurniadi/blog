# Throwing out my Past

I recently purged a significant chunk of my academic past, specifically related
to my philosophy studies from 2018-2022. This included discarding many
books, notebooks, and papers, all of which had become relics of a previous
chapter.

This act mirrors how I also deleted 48 GitHub repositories. It brings a
strange mix of emotions, acknowledging the past while firmly stepping into the
present. While it was not easy, it felt necessary, and I am ready to move
forward.

Feelings feels.

Pasts presents.

But it must be done, and I will move on.
